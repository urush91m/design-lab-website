Built to capture moments beautifully

Design Lab is a photography and videography service brand providing high-quality visual solutions for individuals, events, and brands. We work across lifestyle photography, portraits, product shoots, weddings, corporate events, private events, and other special occasions.

We combine creative direction, visual storytelling, photography, and videography to create content that feels aesthetic, authentic, cinematic, and tailored to each client's vision — whether that's an intimate portrait session or a full brand campaign.

Design Lab currently operates online-first, with no physical studio for walk-in bookings. Clients discover our work on Instagram, share their requirements, and book directly — we work with clients across India and internationally.
